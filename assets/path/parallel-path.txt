RULE
Only projects are atomic. Guides may be split by chapter, but no mandatory chapter or exercise is skipped.
A blocked track waits at the actual consumer block; the other tracks continue.
P09 executes after P03 without renumbering the project or changing the default PATH.

SYNCHRONIZATION SEMANTICS
A → B is a directed prerequisite gate, not a bidirectional rendezvous.
B waits until A is complete; A never waits for B and may continue in its own track.
A completion is an external necessary condition, not a sufficient condition by itself; B also keeps all internal predecessors.
Reaching or running A is insufficient. The assigned chapters, exercises, verification, and P0/P1 closure must be complete.
There are no synchronization-only checkpoint nodes. The prerequisite is recorded on B and drawn directly from A to B.
WEB-G01 through WEB-G06 require only G00 and WEB-internal predecessors; WEB-G07 is the first externally gated WEB block.
